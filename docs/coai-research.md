# COAI/CSOAI Research Findings

## Core Structure

### 33-Agent Byzantine Council
- Uses 12 different AI providers for unbiased assessments
- Vendor-independent, impartial AI safety decisions
- Byzantine consensus mechanism for fault-tolerant decision making

### Key Pipelines & Components

1. **CSO AI** - Central Safety Officer AI (the main coordinator)

2. **Public Watchdog** - Transparent incident database
   - Public reporting
   - Real-time insights
   - Pattern detection

3. **CEASAI Training** - Professional certification program
   - 8-week professional certification
   - 50-question exam, 70% pass rate
   - Creates AI Safety Analysts

4. **SOAI-PDCA Framework** - Continuous Improvement Methodology
   - Plan: Identify AI risks and compliance gaps
   - Do: Implement safety controls
   - Check: Measure effectiveness
   - Act: Adjust and improve

### Compliance Frameworks Covered
- EU AI Act
- NIST AI RMF
- China TC260
- ISO 42001

### Stakeholder Groups
1. **Governments** - Need compliance infrastructure for EU AI Act enforcement
2. **AI Companies** - Need compliance monitoring (50,000+ EU enterprises)
3. **Public** - Incident reporting and transparency
4. **Training/Certification** - CEASAI program for AI Safety Analysts

### Data Flow (The Symbiosis)
1. Public reports AI problems → Watchdog
2. Watchdog data → Council analyzes
3. Council decisions → PDCA cycle starts
4. PDCA improvements → Safer AI
5. Safer AI → More analyst jobs
∞. Better oversight → Safer AI (loop continues)
